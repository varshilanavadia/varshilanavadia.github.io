Favicon and app icon
The mark comes from the supplied vector artwork. The shape is unchanged — what was refined is the execution: a hairline stroke removed, a kinked edge merged, two edges levelled, one taper corrected, five intended-smooth joins made tangent-continuous, and the whole thing renormalised onto a 1000-unit artboard with a true circle. The section below lists each change and what it was before.
There are now two drawings: the main mark, and a 16-pixel master with the ribbon blunted and the counters opened, because a mark this detailed cannot hold its counters at tab size. Both live in Symbol.dc.html and this file defers to it for geometry. What this file settles is the colourway, which crop and which drawing belong in which slot, the sanctioned variants, dark-chrome behaviour, the rasters and manifest, and how the mark behaves on the page.
The colourway is tonal: #134B2E ink on a #D3F0DE ground, at 8.3:1. Both values are the supplied artwork's own.

The ground is absent in the tab on purpose. A #D3F0DE plate on a white active tab is a 1.21:1 boundary — no perceptible edge, so the mark appears to float rather than sit on a tile. With no plate there is nothing to float on, and the mark gets the full 16 pixels instead of 0.86 of them.
That rule holds at every tab size, which settles what actually lands in a tab. Every current browser prefers the SVG favicon and scales it to the strip, so favicon-16.svg — the groundless small master — is what appears at any tab size, HiDPI included. The circle tile never ships to a tab, and neither does the main drawing; the tile's slots are the app icon, the avatar and social, and the main drawing starts above tab scale. The one path that can size-switch is a legacy reach for favicon.ico, and that file carries a circle 32 and a circle 48 with no 16 frame, so a true-16 request downscales the 32. The 32-pixel tile in the set above is that ICO frame, not a live favicon.

Colour
#134B2E
ink
#D3F0DE
ground
Two values, 8.3:1 against each other, both taken from the supplied artwork. Against white the ink is 10.1:1 and the ground is 1.21:1 — which is why the ground is absent from the tab and why the inverse variant exists for placements on white.
The tonal pair does not change between light and dark chrome. A second, inverted colourway would make the identity two marks, and the fixed rasters — app icon, avatar, share image — could not follow a flip anyway. The two named exceptions are the bare mark, which has no ground to preserve, and the Safari mask, which Safari recolours.
The grey variant is 3.5:1 on white. It is sanctioned at tile scale only and must not be used below 64 pixels.
